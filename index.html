Robotic arm engineering notes

A compact view of the current design work before the full case study, so the main direction is understandable directly from the homepage.

Design goal

Develop a six-axis arm architecture that can move from CAD into a testable prototype, with joint layout, link proportions, drive packaging, and service access considered before fabrication.

Current progress

The public artifacts document a 6-DOF CAD layout, arm geometry and torque calculation notes, and a custom cycloidal disk model used to explore compact reduction.

Engineering challenges

The main open questions are torque margin at the shoulder and elbow, reducer and bearing packaging, link stiffness, and whether a cycloidal drive is worth the tolerance and assembly effort.

Learning and next steps

I am using the project to practice front-loaded mechanical reasoning: compare drive concepts, refine joint modules, then prototype one joint before scaling the full arm.
