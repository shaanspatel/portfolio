Project development

  • Simulation reference: the simulation image gives a public reference for the virtual side of the workflow. It is presented as a planning and comparison artifact, not as proof of final robot performance.
  • Lab prototype setup: the workcell image supports a grounded prototype/setup narrative: robot hardware, a table workspace, and target objects or fixtures arranged for lab testing. The page avoids assigning ownership of fixture design or hardware changes beyond what is visible here.
  • Validation capture: the validation image shows that the workflow included capture or review of experimental outputs. Public wording remains qualitative until any protocol, sample size, measured result, or timing detail is cleared through lab records.
  • Documentation boundaries: because this is active research work, the public version avoids private lab details, unreleased results, and unsupported status claims. The emphasis is on careful engineering communication: what can be shown, what it supports, and what still needs verification.
