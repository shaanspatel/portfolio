Overview

This OH-MIND Lab project connects simulation, physical prototype setup, and validation imagery for a research submission. The page should read as a research case study: what was modeled, how the robot workspace was configured, and how validation evidence was collected.

Problem / Motivation

Robotics research needs a bridge between simulated assumptions and the constraints of a real robot testbed. This project focuses on making that bridge visible through simulation scenes, lab setup, prototype/target objects, and validation snapshots that can support a research narrative without overstating final findings.

Engineering Constraints

The work is constrained by research assumptions, calibration or registration quality, sensing and lighting conditions, robot workspace limits, and the need for repeatable validation evidence. Any quantitative accuracy, success-rate, or timing results should stay out of the public page until pulled from approved lab records or the submission.

Design Approach

The approach is to use simulation to define or preview robot-environment interaction, then align the physical setup so the lab capture can test the same ideas. The prototype visuals suggest an iterative loop between virtual scenes, workspace setup, and validation snapshots.
