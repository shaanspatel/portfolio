Overview

This project is an in-progress 6-DOF robotic arm development effort. The current documented phase is mechanical design: defining the arm architecture, laying out joint axes, checking torque paths, exploring drivetrain options, and packaging the mechanism before committing to fabrication.

My role / current status

My current work is focused on the mechanical side of the arm: CAD layout, joint architecture, preliminary torque calculations, drivetrain exploration, and packaging. Electronics, controls, fabrication, testing, and AI/ML integration are planned future phases and are not presented here as complete.

Project development

The project is being developed from the mechanical architecture outward so that later electrical and controls decisions have a clear mechanical baseline to support.

  • Arm architecture: laying out the base, shoulder, elbow, wrist, and end-effector degrees of freedom.
  • CAD packaging: organizing link geometry, joint envelopes, fastener access, and drivetrain space.
  • Preliminary calculations: estimating joint torque needs and using those estimates to guide drivetrain decisions.
  • Drivetrain exploration: comparing compact reduction concepts, including cycloidal-drive geometry, before selecting hardware.
  • Future integration: leaving room in the project plan for electronics, controls, fabrication, testing, and AI/ML work after the mechanical baseline is better defined.

Joint architecture

The arm is being planned around a 6-DOF layout so the mechanical structure can support useful positioning and orientation work later. At this stage, the documented work is about joint order, axis placement, link relationships, packaging space, and how loads would move through the arm.

CAD and packaging

The CAD work is being used to turn the arm concept into a manufacturable mechanical layout. Current packaging questions include actuator placement, link clearances, joint module size, service access, and whether the drivetrain concepts fit inside realistic joint envelopes.

Drivetrain exploration

The drivetrain work is exploratory. The cycloidal disk model documents one reduction concept being considered for compact joint packaging, but it does not represent a validated transmission or tested joint module yet.

Torque calculations

The calculation work is preliminary and used for design direction, not final performance claims. These estimates help identify which joints are likely to drive actuator selection, reduction ratio choices, and packaging constraints in later revisions.
